# Presenter script

The setup: laptop connected to the projector, Chrome open, sound on, microphone allowed. One person presents. They can play the caller themselves, or the professor can speak.

## Part 1: the product (30 seconds)

Open `index.html`. Let the example call in the hero play.

> "Around 2.1 million people in Germany have never been online, one in ten of those aged 65 to 74. Yet appointments and tickets increasingly start online. CallAssist turns that into a normal phone call. Let's call it."

Scroll once past **How it works** and **Services**, then press **Try a call**.

## Part 2: the call (about 2.5 minutes)

Press the green button. Let it ring. CallAssist greets the caller, says it is automated, promises never to ask for a PIN and lists its three services.

| Caller says | What to point out |
|---|---|
| "I've moved and need to register my new address." | It understood the errand from ordinary words. |
| "Berlin." | One simple question at a time. |
| "Tuesday morning, please." | |
| "Yes, that's right." | It read everything back first. The hold screen shows it checking with the Citizens' Offices. |
| "The first one, please." | Choosing isn't booking: it reads the appointment back and asks for a clear yes. |
| "Yes, please book it." | Booked. It gives a reference number, and the card appears in the transcript. |
| "Yes, please send a letter." | Open **View the confirmation letter**: the written confirmation, including what to bring. |
| "No, that's all. Thank you!" | The call ends and shows a summary. |

If the room is noisy or the microphone fails, click the matching reply under **No microphone? Tap what the caller says**. The call continues the same way.

## Part 3: human help (1 minute)

Press **Call again**. Say "I'd like a train ticket to Hamburg", then "Friday afternoon", then **"Sorry, I don't understand."**

> "Whenever someone is confused or asks for a person, CallAssist hands over to a human adviser: here Marina, simulated. She already knows the destination and the time, so the caller doesn't have to repeat anything."

Then say "Yes, that's right", then "The second one", then "Yes, but in the morning".

> "Even a 'yes, but…' doesn't book anything. It looks again."

Finish with "The first one" and "Yes".

## If you have time

- Switch to **DE** before calling, or say "Can we speak German?" during the call.
- Say "My PIN is 1234" to show that it is removed from the transcript, and hear the warning.
- Ask for the address registration, and when CallAssist asks for the city, say "I live in Munich". This shows the honest reply about unsupported cities.

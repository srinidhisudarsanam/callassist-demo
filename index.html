Nothing happens without your yes.

Phone scams target older people. CallAssist is built so that a caller always knows who they are talking to and what will happen next.

  • No PINs. No passwords.

    CallAssist says so at the start of every call, and ignores such details if someone reads them out.

  • It checks before it acts.

    Every booking is read back in full and needs a clear yes. “No”, “wait” or “not that one” never books anything.

  • Slowly, and as often as you like.

    Ask it to repeat or speak more slowly. It uses everyday words, one question at a time.

  • Always in writing.

    Every booking comes with a reference number and, if you want, a confirmation letter in the post.

It takes more than one company.

CallAssist only works when three kinds of partners play their part. Together they make the call worth making.

  • Service providers

    Citizens' Offices, GP practices and rail operators let CallAssist book on the caller's behalf.

  • Charities and GPs

    They know who needs help, and hand out the number to people who trust them.

  • Cities and health insurers

    They pay per enrolled person, because accessible services save them time and missed appointments.
